# Privacy Policy for HAIO - Hide AI Overview

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026
**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

HAIO - Hide AI Overview ("HAIO", "the extension") is a browser extension that hides the AI Overview section on Google Search results and lets you show or hide it with a button.

## Summary

HAIO does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Everything it does happens inside your browser.

## Information HAIO collects

None. Specifically, HAIO does not collect or transmit:

- your search queries or browsing history
- the content of any web page, including Google Search results
- personal information of any kind, such as your name, email address, or location
- identifiers, cookies, or usage statistics

## Information stored on your device

HAIO saves one setting: whether the AI Overview should be hidden by default (the switch in the extension's popup). It is stored in your browser's local extension storage on your own device. It is never sent anywhere, and it is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## How HAIO works with web pages

HAIO runs a script only on Google Search results pages (`google.com/search` and `www.google.com/search`). The script looks for the AI Overview section on the page, hides it, and adds a button that lets you reveal it. This all happens locally in your browser. HAIO does not read your search queries or send any page content anywhere, and it does not run on any other website.

## Permissions

- **Storage:** used only to remember the single setting described above.
- **Access to Google Search results pages:** used only to hide and show the AI Overview section on those pages, as described above.

HAIO requests no other permissions.

## Third parties

HAIO does not share data with anyone, because it does not collect any. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code, makes no requests to external servers, and does not load or run remotely hosted code. Your use of Google Search itself is covered by Google's own privacy policy, and HAIO does not send anything to Google.

## Compliance with store policies

HAIO does not handle user data, so it does not use or transfer user data for any purpose, and its use of any information would adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Children's privacy

HAIO does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Open source

HAIO's source code is public, so you can verify what it does: https://github.com/caffeine98/hideAIOverview

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the project repository with a new "last updated" date. Because HAIO collects no data, I do not expect significant changes.
